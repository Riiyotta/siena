# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - reference / generated modes (PageSpec-breaking: `pageSpecVersion` 2.0.0)

A generator could reuse the source's brand: the Siena wordmark and glyph, customer / partner / investor logos, real people's
photos, the commissioned artwork, the source's product screenshots and video, the real YouTube id, the SOC 2 badge, the
`siena.cx` links and the verified testimonial set were all valid output of the contract. A page for another brand had no way
to say "not Siena" and nothing checked that it was not.

- **`mode` (required closed enum `reference` | `generated`)** on every PageSpec: schema (`pagespec.schema.json` generated by
  `schema/build_schema.py`, with a reference `node` and a `nodeGenerated` definition selected by `mode`), allowlist
  (`settablePageProperties`), both existing examples (now `reference`), the synthesized controls, README. A PageSpec without
  `mode` is invalid, hence `pageSpecVersion` 1.1.0 -> 2.0.0 (the repository itself goes 1.1.0 -> 1.2.0: minor, contract additions).
- **`assets/asset-roles.json`** 1.1.0: every role has a closed `availability` (`reference-only` 10 | `blocked-for-generation` 6 |
  `generation-safe` 10), `generatedModeGuidance` and (unless generation-safe) `generationSafeSubstitutes`. Nine new
  generation-safe roles (17 -> 26): `logo.placeholder`, `logo.user-supplied`, `avatar.placeholder`, `photo.user-supplied`,
  `illustration.generated`, `product.ui-generated`, `animation.generated`, `video.user-supplied`, `icon.generated`.
  Availability is **pinned in `verify_all.py`** (`PINNED_AVAILABILITY`), independent of the registry. `badge.compliance`
  (a certification mark) is the only role with no substitute: omit the slot.
- **Sections**: all 86 contracts carry `generatedAssetRoles` (derived from the reference slots through the substitutes; verified
  against the derivation by `validate_section_contract` / check 7); `shell.navbar`, `shell.lab-nav` and `shell.footer` carry
  `generatedOverrides` (required `brand`, `linkSet: placeholder`; footer drops `complianceBadge`) because their Siena wordmark
  and link set were implicit chrome with no PageSpec field. Generated-mode twin content models also: `assetRef` only under
  `placeholder/`, `generated/`, `user-supplied/`; hrefs only internal or reserved `.example`/`.invalid`; quotes and `copySource`
  `generated`; `speaker` `agent` instead of `siena`; icon and video slots lose `codepoint` / `videoId`.
- **`sourceBrand` registry** (`extraction/build_source_brand.py`, derived from `src/`, `CLONE_SPEC.md`, `specs/`, `public/`,
  `recon/`): brand token, 10 URL host/path patterns + 6 personal handles, 74 customer / partner / investor name patterns
  (13 ambiguous English-word names listed, not matched in text), 34 people, 23 third-party platforms (warn only), 10,109 copy
  shingles and 1,130 media-file stems (hashes only, in `assets/source-copy-hashes.json`). **No human reviewed these lists.**
- **Validator** (`schema/semantic_validate.py`): 12 `GENERATED_MODE_*` rules (`REFERENCE_ASSET`, `ROLE`, `ASSET_REF`, `SOURCE_BRAND`,
  `SOURCE_URL`, `EXTERNAL_URL`, `SOURCE_PERSON`, `SOURCE_CUSTOMER`, `SOURCE_COPY`, `QUOTE`, `PLACEHOLDER_COPY`, `THIRD_PARTY` warn);
  generated pages may use any non-brand route (constraints from the template's first route), skip the homepage-testimonial-set
  and nav-CTA text rules, and need no modelled-route links. Reference mode is unchanged and still validates.
- **Examples**: four fictional-brand generated PageSpecs ("Brightloop"): `schema/examples/generated.template.{home,product,conversation-detail,legal}.pagespec.json`.
- **Tests**: adversarial suite 84 -> 172 PageSpec mutations (mode field, logo/brand leakage, source copy, screenshot/media reuse,
  source URL, person / customer identity), +10 contract mutations (38), 14 direct cases (including generated-layer code paths the
  schema blocks first and hash-patched registry tests), 65 controls (4 generated examples, 5 generated edits that must pass,
  27 synthesized generated controls, one per template). `verify_all.py` check 15 (generated mode: pinned source-identity probes,
  8 canary mutations, registry re-derivation from `src/`, example coverage) and 30 new `prove_drift.py` injections (48 -> 78),
  including "the validator stops enforcing generated mode".
- Found while testing: the first draft of an original example sentence reproduced a source phrase ("replies in your brand voice");
  the shingle rule rejected it and the sentence was rewritten.
- Not done: paraphrase detection; copy under 5 words; the `siena` token in template / section / role ids and two key names
  (contract vocabulary, documented as exempt); a renderer's use of a role is not checked; real third-party product names only warn.

## 1.1.0 - page decomposition, wrapper gate, retrieval eligibility

- Measured the real rendered height of every template node at 1440px on the built clone (35 routes, 364 section
  instances; `extraction/measure_heights.mjs`, `extraction/height-probes.json`, `measured-values.json` `sectionHeights`).
  Tallest hero 1552px (`/partner-with-siena`); no hero over 1800px.
- New `extraction/wrapper_gate.py` (verify_all check 13): hero >1800px warns, >3000px fails unless reviewed-whitelisted
  (reviewedBy, justification >= 30 chars, maxHeightPx above the measurement); a section holding >50% of a page warns
  (4 reviewed in `shareReviewed`); one H1 per page inside the hero (or a content header on hero-less templates);
  missing measurement is an error; template route coherence (Jaccard >= 0.6); proven by 25 adversarial gate cases and
  6 drift cases.
- Decomposed `content.conversation-article` into 6 sections (header, video embed, summary, chapter index, one
  transcript chapter per chapter, FAQ), `content.legal-document` into `content.legal-header` + `content.legal-section`
  (one per heading-bounded part) and `content.conversation-index` into `content.index-header` +
  `content.conversation-grid`. 79 -> 86 sections. New graph rule `CHAPTER_PARITY` (27 -> 28 rules). Templates
  `conversations-index`, `conversation-detail`, `legal` and `insights-landing`, graph adjacency, allowlist, asset-role
  `usedBy`, quirks, example PageSpecs, `EMBED_ALLOWLIST` (now `media.video-embed`), IA files and README updated together.
- `feature.agent-promo` renamed `cta.agent-promo` and reclassified as conversion (CTA copy no longer sits in a feature).
- Every section contract now carries `retrievalEligible` (closed boolean; `false` + `retrievalIneligibleReason` for
  `overlay.partner-modal` and `quirk.phone-empty-block`), a closed `semantic {id, category}` and a closed
  `source {id, tag, citation, file, lines}`. New `schema/section-contract.schema.json` and
  `schema/section-classification.json`; `semantic_validate.validate_section_contract` and verify_all check 12 enforce
  them (retrieval flag pinned in five places, source lines re-read from the real files, class policy).
- verify_all gains check 12 (section contracts), 13 (wrapper gate) and 14 (IA parity against `ia.json` / `IA.md` /
  `matrix.csv` when beside the repo); adversarial suite 74 -> 84 PageSpec mutations plus 28 contract mutations and 25
  gate cases; drift proof 27 -> 48 injected defects.
- Manifest/allowlist/section registry/pageSpec versions 1.0.0 -> 1.1.0; counts gain `retrievalIneligibleSections`,
  `measuredRoutes`, `measuredSectionInstances`.
- Not measured: the visual-parity gate (no live-site capture available); tablet widths.

## 1.0.0 - initial build

- Built from scratch (design-repo-extraction skill, BUILD-GUIDE order): 298 tokens
  (22 typography roles, 20 motion recipes), 12 primitives,
  24 components, 79 sections, 27 templates covering all 35 routes 1:1,
  27 graph rules, 17 asset roles, 354 citations, 24 verified-quote hashes,
  10 registered live-site quirks.
- Foundation colours/sizes/radii backed by a usage histogram computed from the 42 recon
  computed-style dumps (`extraction/style-histogram.json`); hover/focus/gradient values cite specs.
- Semantic validator cross-references every PageSpec against its declared template's real
  node list keyed on (section, variant); graph rule ids and validator code are asserted equal.
- Adversarial suite: hand-written mutations + one synthesized control per template.
- Drift proof (`extraction/prove_drift.py`) injects allowlist, citation, count, version,
  entry-point, policy, schema, catalog, histogram-claim, pinned-policy, route and
  machine-path defects into a scratch copy and requires each to fail.
- Product A/B templates were authored from specs while those pages were still being built,
  then re-checked against current `src/` (citations re-resolved) before release.
